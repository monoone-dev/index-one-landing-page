Ivy skills change how Ivy writes: the style and form of your meeting notes and of its answers in Ask. A skill never changes what a note says. Ivy still sticks to what was said in the meeting, keeps decisions and commitments as they were, and writes in the language you chose. Skills arrived in [3.19](/changelog/#v3.19.0).

## Before you start

- **Sign in.** Ivy skills come from the IndexOne catalog, so you browse and install them with your IndexOne account. Signed out, no skill applies.
- **Choose your AI.** Skills are designed to guide notes and answers written with Ivy in the cloud. Ivy on this Mac also receives your skills, but its smaller model may not follow them reliably. The AI for each place is set in Settings › AI Models; see [AI models](/docs/ai-models/).

## Browse skills

Open **Plugins** in the left rail. In **Discover**, the **Ivy skills** filter shows only skills; **All** shows plugins and skills together. Open a skill to see its page:

- **What it does**, in a few lines.
- **See the difference**: the same sample meeting, once without the skill and once with it.
- Where it is installed, if it is.

A skill has nothing to set up. Once it is on, it works by itself.

## Install a skill

Choose **Install** and pick where it should work: **On this Mac**, **Personal** or one of your organizations. A skill works only in the places you install it, and you can add it to more places later. Only an organization's admins can install skills for that organization, and then they apply to every member.

Notes already written don't change. To apply your skills to an older note, use **Rewrite** on the note, described below.

## Your skills in this place

At the top of Plugins, **Your skills in** (On this Mac, Personal or the organization) lists the skills in force where you are working, with who turned each one on:

- **You**: a skill you installed. Turn it off with its switch.
- **Organization … · for everyone**: an organization skill you can turn off for everyone, because you are an admin.
- **Organization … · turned on by an admin**: an organization skill shown with a lock. Only its admins can turn it off.

## Where you see skills at work

Skills show up as small chips with Ivy's icon:

- **The recording bar**: "The note will be written by Ivy with skills:" lists the skills of the place you are recording to. The list stays the same for the whole recording.
- **A meeting note**: "Written by Ivy with skills:" names the skills a note was written with. **Rewrite** writes the note again with the skills that are on today.
- **Ask Ivy**: next to the question field, the chips name the skills that shape Ivy's answers there. Click one to open your skills in Plugins.

A meeting uses the skills of the place it lives in, and a recording uses those of the place you record to.
