IndexOne has two kinds of storage. Settings › Storage is about the disk on this Mac. Settings › Cloud storage is about what you keep in your IndexOne account. Notes and transcripts are never deleted by either; only recording files and other files count.

## Storage on this Mac

Settings › Storage shows how much disk your recordings use, split into audio and screen video, with **Reveal in Finder**.

- **Storage limit (GB)**: the most disk your recordings may use. Leave it blank for no limit. Only audio and screen video count; notes and transcripts are always kept.
- **Automatically delete old recordings**: when you are over the limit, IndexOne deletes the oldest recordings' screen video first, then their audio. Your notes and transcripts are never deleted, and recordings in locked folders are never touched.
- **Free up space now**: deletes the oldest recordings' screen video, then audio, down to your limit right away, after you confirm. It needs a limit, and it cannot be undone.

When you are working in Personal or an organization, the page also has **This workspace on this Mac**. Turn on **Keep recordings on this Mac** to download every recording of that workspace when there is room, so it plays without a connection. Turning it off keeps what is already here. Recordings you play are kept on this Mac either way.

## Recordings to recover

If a recording could not be turned into a meeting, its original audio is kept. **Recordings to recover** at the top of Settings › Storage lists these recordings. For each one you can:

- **Recover** it, so IndexOne transcribes it and writes the note.
- **Export as WAV**, which saves the audio to Downloads. Exporting never changes the original.

A failed recovery never deletes or changes your audio. It is removed only after a note is made.

## Cloud storage in your account

While you are signed in, Settings › Cloud storage shows everything you have recorded or uploaded to IndexOne, in every space and organization. Files that only live on your Mac do not count.

- How much of your storage you use, by category (recordings, uploaded files, attachments, shared files and trash) and by organization.
- **Your files**, sorted largest or newest first. Select files and choose **Delete files**, or empty the trash in one step. Deleting files never deletes notes or transcripts; the note shows that the file was deleted, when and by whom.
- **Keep recording files**: how long recording files are kept. After that time they are deleted automatically; transcripts, notes and attachments stay. Shortening it applies to existing recordings too, and IndexOne shows what gets deleted before you confirm. Longer retention comes with paid plans.

## Organization storage

Choose an organization in the space switcher, then open Settings › Organization › Storage. It shows how much storage the organization uses and how much of it you uploaded. Owners and admins also see **Who uploaded what**, sorted by size or name. Files you upload to an organization count toward your storage.

## When storage fills up

- At 95%, IndexOne tells you that your storage is almost full.
- Over your limit, you cannot upload new files. Recording audio still works until the reserve runs out.
- When your storage is full, new recordings and uploads to your account pause until you free up space. Your notes and transcripts stay.

These messages offer **Free up space**, so you can delete files you no longer need. When you record to Personal or an organization, the Record screen also warns you before your storage fills up; see [Recording](/docs/recording/).
