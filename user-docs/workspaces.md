On this Mac keeps everything you record and write in one tree of workspaces and folders. This page explains the sidebar, how items get filed, the Trash, and locking a workspace.

![The sidebar with the space switcher set to On this Mac, the Inbox with three meetings, and the Workspaces tree with Product, Engineering, Clients and a locked workspace](shot:hero-spaces "The sidebar of On this Mac")

## The space switcher

The menu above the sidebar shows where you are working: **On this Mac**, **Personal** or one of your organizations. Click it to open the panel. It shows the current place with a short line about where its notes live, the other places you can switch to, and, when you are signed in, **New organization** and **Join organization**. Signed out, it offers **Sign in to open your workspaces**.

This page is about On this Mac. Personal and organizations have their own spaces and folders: see [Organizations and Personal](/docs/organizations/).

## The sidebar

- **Inbox** holds what you have not filed yet, such as a new recording. Drag an item onto the Inbox to unfile it.
- **Workspaces** is your tree. A workspace holds folders, and folders can hold folders. A folder holds your recordings, notes, tasks and projects side by side, so a meeting's note sits next to the work it belongs to. Use **+** next to Workspaces to create a workspace or a folder.
- At the bottom: **Capture** opens the Record screen, the note button creates a **Quick note**, and **+** creates a new note, project or reminder. When some protected items are unlocked, the footer shows how many, with **Lock all**.

The slim rail on the far left has **Search** (`⌘K`), **Smart organize** and **Browse** at the top, and **Ask Ivy**, **Plugins**, **Send feedback**, **Settings** and your account at the bottom. When you collapse the sidebar, Capture and Quick note move to the rail.

## The Browse menu

**Browse** lists everything by kind, across all folders:

| Group | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Work | Meetings, Processing queue, Notes, Tasks, Projects, Reminders |
| Intelligence | Ivy |
| Insights | Analytics, Graph, People |
| Storage | Trash |

## Filing

Drag an item where it belongs, or use **Move…** in its menu.

**Smart organize** (in the rail) reorganizes the whole library from an instruction in your own words. It plans the change, creating, renaming and moving folders, workspaces and projects, and shows every step in a preview. Only what you accept is applied, and **Undo reorganization** puts it back. A plan never deletes a project. If your AI for On this Mac is Claude Code or Codex, Smart organize asks once for permission to send titles, labels and folder names to it.

After you apply a plan, its folders become rules for new items. **Automatic filing** in Settings › Notes files new notes and recordings that have no folder yet:

- **Filing**: **Off**, **Trial** (shows what it would have filed, without moving anything) or **On**.
- **When the rules can't place an item**: **Rules only** leaves the item where it is, and nothing is sent anywhere. **Rules, then ask the model** asks your AI where the item belongs.

Anything you put in a folder yourself is never moved.

## The Trash

Deleted items go to Browse › **Trash** first. **Restore** puts one back, **Delete for good** removes it, and **Empty Trash** removes everything. Items are kept for 30 days by default; you can choose 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days. Items from a locked folder can only be seen or restored after you unlock it.

## Locking a workspace

Choose **Protect…** on a workspace, folder, note or meeting to lock it with Touch ID or your Mac password. Everything inside is encrypted and hidden from search, Ivy and connected apps until you unlock it. A locked workspace shows only its name in the sidebar, because you need the name to unlock it: no counts and no items. Locked folders always stay on this Mac.

Unlocked items lock again after a period of inactivity, or all at once with **Lock all**. See [What a lock protects](/docs/lock-model/) and [App lock](/docs/app-lock/).

## Old shared notes

If you used the end-to-end encrypted sharing of earlier versions, everything that was shared with you, and every old encrypted organization on this Mac, is in **On this Mac › Shared**. See [Organizations and Personal](/docs/organizations/#what-happened-to-old-shared-notes).
