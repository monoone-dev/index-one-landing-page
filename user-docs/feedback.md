You can send a bug, an idea or anything else straight to the people building IndexOne, from inside the app. Every report gets a number, and you can follow its status and the team's replies in Settings › Feedback.

## Send a report

Open **Send feedback** with the speech-bubble button at the bottom of the left rail, or choose **Send feedback…** in the IndexOne menu-bar menu. Then:

1. Pick what it is: **Bug** (something is broken), **Idea** (something is missing) or **Other**.
2. Give it a **Title**, a short summary of up to 80 characters.
3. Describe it in the text field. For a bug: what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
4. Add images if they help, then choose **Send**.

If you are signed out, you can leave an email address for a reply. It is optional; you can follow the report in Settings › Feedback without it.

When a meeting cannot be opened, its error screen offers **Report this**, which opens the same form.

## What is attached, and what never is

You decide what goes with a report:

- **Screenshot of this window.** For a bug, IndexOne adds a screenshot of the IndexOne window. It is sent only if you leave it in the form; remove it if you do not want it.
- **Images.** Up to three in all, PNG, JPEG or WebP, each up to 5 MB. Paste one or choose **Add image**.
- **Technical details.** For a bug only, behind the **Include technical details** switch. The form lists exactly what is sent, nothing else: the app version, macOS version and Mac model; the screen you were on; the app language; the organization you were working in, as an ID only; and an error code if there was one.

Your notes, recordings and transcripts are never attached.

## After you send

IndexOne shows the report's permanent number, such as `IO-B-41`. Choose **Copy number** and quote it when you write to the team. If sending fails, your report stays in the form, nothing is lost, and you can try again.

## Settings › Feedback

Settings › Feedback, in the Help group, lists everything you sent next to the report you selected. Filter it with **All**, **In progress** and **Completed**. In a narrow window, the list and the report are shown one above the other.

Each report shows its number, its title (older reports without one show their first sentence) and its status. The status words fit the kind of report:

| Status | Bug | Idea | Other |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Just sent | Received | Received | Received |
| Accepted | Planned | Planned | Planned |
| Being worked on | In progress | In progress | In progress |
| Finished | Fixed | Shipped | Done |
| Not taken up | Closed | Not planned | Closed |

When a fix ships, the report says which version has it and whether you already have it or need to update.

## Replies

The team's reply appears as a message under your report, with its formatting, such as lists, links and emphasis, and any images attached to it. A new reply puts a dot on the feedback button in the left rail and a badge on Feedback in Settings.
