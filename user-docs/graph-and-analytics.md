As you record and write, IndexOne recognizes the people and projects you talk about and connects them into a graph, on your Mac. Browse › Graph and Browse › People let you explore it, the Ivy page draws everything as one map, and Browse › Analytics shows how much you meet and exactly what has left your Mac.

## The graph builds itself

Each meeting note names the people and projects it mentions. IndexOne links them across meetings automatically, with no setup: open a person or a project and you see every meeting they came up in. **Link people & projects** in a meeting's More menu links the people and projects in its note.

The graph only shows what you can open. Items in a locked folder are hidden until you unlock it, and the page says when something is hidden. On this Mac shows only meetings that are not in an organization.

## Graph

Browse › **Graph** lists the people and projects across your meetings, with how often each one is mentioned.

![The Graph page with People and Projects, each with its mention count](shot:graph "Browse › Graph")

- **Search people and projects…** narrows the list.
- **All**, **People** and **Projects** filter by kind.
- **Sort** by **Most mentioned** or **Name**.

Open one to see its details: the meetings it is **Mentioned in**, the entities it appears with, and a decision ledger of **Changes** over the last 90 days or all time. Each entry links to its source meeting. See [Receipts](/docs/receipts/).

## People

Browse › **People** lists everyone across your meetings: how recently you talked, how many open commitments and known facts they have. Open a person for their dossier:

- **Timeline**: the meetings that mention them.
- **Who owes what**: open commitments, with due dates.
- **Facts**: what IndexOne knows about them now, and **What changed** over time.
- **Also appears with**: the people and projects they come up with most.

## The full Ivy graph

On the Ivy page (Browse › Ivy), **Full Ivy graph** draws everything, meetings, notes, documents, people and projects, as one map.

![The full Ivy graph with clusters of meetings, notes, documents and people](shot:ivy-graph "The full Ivy graph in the Clusters layout")

- **Layers** or **Clusters** arranges the map.
- **Nodes** shows or hides People & projects, Meetings, Notes and Documents.
- **Links** shows or hides each kind of connection: Co-occurrence, Mentions, Wikilinks, Companion, Manual and Semantic, plus **Suggested** links.
- Size shows how connected an item is and line weight shows how strong a link is. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click to focus and double-click to open. **List** shows the same items as a list, and **Fit** fits the map to the window.

The graph can be used from the keyboard too: arrow keys move to the nearest item, Page Up and Page Down step through all of them, Enter opens the selected one and Escape clears the selection.

## Analytics

Browse › **Analytics** sums up your meetings:

- **Total meetings**, **Total time**, **This week** and **Avg length**.
- **Activity**: meetings per day over the last 30 days.
- **By status**, **Longest session**, **Notes created** and **Recording since**.
- **Topic threads**: subjects that come back across meetings.

### Weekly digest

Pick 7 or 30 days and choose **Generate digest** for a written summary of that period. It is made by the AI chosen for the workspace, with the usual redaction.

### What leaves this Mac

This is the record behind redaction: every call IndexOne made to a cloud AI, over 7, 30 or 90 days.

- **Cloud calls** and **Tokens sent** (a token is roughly ¾ of a word).
- **Emails, phones and cards removed** before sending, and restored in your notes, plus how many names were masked when name masking is on.
- Breakdowns **By model** and **By day**, and the **Recent calls**, including any images sent.
- A call sent without name masking is marked **Names not masked**. A call sent during a recording notes that only pattern redaction applied, because the on-device name model was paused.

When nothing was sent, it says that everything stayed on this Mac. See [Redaction](/docs/redaction/) and [Data flow](/docs/data-flow/).
