Tasks and reminders are two different things on purpose. Tasks are shared work that belongs to an organization. Reminders are your own follow-ups, kept in On this Mac.

## Tasks

Tasks live in an organization, so they need you to be signed in to your IndexOne account. Open Browse › **Tasks** while an organization is selected to see the work of that organization, shared with the members of each space.

- Find a task by name, filter by space, and switch between **Open**, **To do**, **In progress**, **Done** and **All**.
- A task has a title, a description, a **Status** (To do, In progress or Done) and a **Due** date.
- Under **Subtasks**, keep a checklist of smaller steps.
- Add images to a task, and link related tasks.
- Choose **New task** to create one in a space of the organization.

Deleted tasks go to the organization's Trash. A task in the Trash can only be restored.

![The Tasks screen with a task open, showing its status In progress, a due date and a checklist of subtasks](shot:tasks "A task with its status, due date and subtasks")

> [!NOTE]
> In On this Mac, Tasks shows the tasks from the organizations of earlier IndexOne versions, kept on this Mac. New shared work belongs in one of your organizations; see [Organizations and Personal](/docs/organizations/).

## Reminders

Reminders are part of On this Mac and are stored on this Mac. Organizations and Personal don't have reminders. Open them from Browse › **Reminders**.

![The Reminders screen with the Inbox tab open, showing two overdue reminders, each linked to the meeting it came from](shot:reminders "Reminders, each linked to the meeting it came from")

The tabs:

- **Inbox**: reminders that are **Overdue** or **Due now**. They stay until you complete or dismiss them.
- **Upcoming**: reminders that are not due yet.
- **Completed**: finished reminders.

### Create a reminder

Choose **New reminder** on the Reminders screen, or **+** at the bottom of the sidebar. A reminder has:

- a **Title** and, if you like, **Details**,
- a **Date** and **Time**,
- **Repeat**, every so many days, weeks, months or years,
- **Sources**: up to 20 notes or meetings it belongs to.

Each reminder keeps a link back to its sources, so a follow-up always says where it came from. Reminders you create from a note stay attached to that note and show up in your Reminders inbox when they come due.

From a meeting note, **Add to Reminders** on an action item turns it into a reminder.

### Smart reminders

On a meeting or a note, **Smart reminders** suggests follow-ups from what was actually said or written. Review each suggestion: create it as a reminder, or dismiss it. IndexOne never creates suggestions as reminders automatically.
