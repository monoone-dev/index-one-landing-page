*Originally released on 2026-11-27.*

### New

- Import a meeting transcript. A transcript exported from Teams, Zoom or Google Meet, a subtitle file (.vtt or .srt) or a plain-text transcript becomes a new meeting with its timestamps and speakers, and IndexOne writes the note for it.

### Fixes

- Text from Word documents keeps characters such as "&", "<" and ">" instead of dropping them.

### Downloads

IndexOne requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 13.4 or later.
