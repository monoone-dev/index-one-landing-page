*Originally released on 2026-10-07.*

Meetings can now be translated: the transcript, the note and the live captions, always next to the originals.

### Meeting translation

- A meeting's transcript and note can be translated into English, Polish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Dutch, Chinese or Japanese. The original stays untouched, and a switch above the text moves between the original and each translation.
- In a translated transcript, "Show original" puts the original line under each translated one.
- Translations are saved with the meeting. When the original changes, the translation is marked as outdated and can be translated again with one click.
- Translations are locked and unlocked together with their folder, like the rest of the meeting.
- Translation uses the AI engine chosen for notes, with the same privacy settings.

### Live translation

- During a recording, live captions can be translated line by line, with each translation shown under the original line.
- After a recording with live translation on, the final transcript is translated automatically.
- Live translation needs a cloud Live engine. With an on-device Live model it shows as unavailable during the recording, and the transcript is translated after the recording instead.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
