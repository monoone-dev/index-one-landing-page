## Developer mode, and a log you can actually read

Turn on **developer mode** in Settings and IndexOne keeps a readable log of what the app
is doing. A row expands into the whole entry, one filter bar narrows it down, and
entries are kept for 24 hours and then genuinely deleted — not trimmed, not archived.

Useful when something misbehaves and "it just didn't work" is not a bug report anyone
can act on.

## Settings is a modal now

Settings used to be a pane wedged beside the app. It is now a modal over the window,
starting below the window-button band, with the selected section actually reading as
selected and the rail's scroll fade behaving at the last section.

## Also

The landing page and README were still describing the shell 2.2.0 removed — Spaces is
called **Workspaces** everywhere now, and the sentence about "a persistent icon rail
plus a contextual panel" is gone, because that layout is. Trash, which shipped in 2.2.0
and was mentioned nowhere, is now described with its real 30-day retention.
