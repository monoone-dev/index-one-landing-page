*Originally released on 2026-10-06.*

IndexOne now speaks nine languages, a forgotten sharing password can be reset with the recovery phrase, Settings is reorganized, and Ivy answers while a recording is running.

### Languages

- The app is available in English, Polish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese, the same languages as the website.
- Settings → Appearance has a Language picker. By default the app follows the macOS language and falls back to English.
- The translations are machine-generated and have not yet been reviewed by native speakers.

### Sharing account

- "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen resets the password with your recovery phrase. Your account key and every share stay as they are.
- A reset without the recovery phrase is not available yet, and the app says so.
- The sign-in dialog is redesigned: one sign-in screen with a link to create an account, clearer steps, and the recovery phrase shown as a numbered word grid.

### Settings

- Settings is regrouped into smaller sections: Ivy has its own section, Editor moved into Appearance, and Obsidian into General.
- Health Check groups its results into Needs attention, Working and Optional.
- Privacy, Connectors, Note assistant and the AI choice cards are rebuilt, with a toggle per group and a warning when the default engine is unavailable.

### Ivy during a recording

- Ask, note, project and meeting chats answer while a recording is running instead of failing, and stopping a recording no longer waits for an answer in progress.
- If you use on-device name masking, it can't run next to live transcription. Ivy now stops before sending anything and offers to send that one question without name masking, or to ask again after the recording. Nothing is sent unmasked without your choice.
- An on-device Ask model, or a model server on this Mac, is paused for the recording and Ivy says so instead of showing an error.
- Search inside Ask falls back to keyword search while the on-device embedder is paused.
- During a recording at most two Ivy questions run at a time, and Claude Code or Codex run at lower priority, so the transcript keeps up.
- With Claude Code, Ask no longer answers "No such tool available".

### Fixes

- A few labels that were in Polish for everyone now read in English.
- The default accent swatch shows the theme's real colour, Ivy is capitalised everywhere, and the live transcript panel matches the editor.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
