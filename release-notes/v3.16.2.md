*Originally released on 2026-11-11.*

A recording saved to Personal or to an organization space no longer leaves a copy on this Mac, and deleting a meeting, note or folder with related notes works again.

### Recording to an organization

- When you save a recording to Personal or to a space in an organization, its copy on this Mac is now removed once the recording has arrived. Before, the copy was left behind, and you could not rename or edit it.
- Copies left behind by 3.16.0 and 3.16.1 are cleaned up when this version starts.
- Nothing is lost: the recording is in its space.

### Deleting

- Deleting a meeting, note or folder that is linked to related notes no longer fails with an error. This could happen since September.
- Nothing was lost: a meeting, note or folder that could not be deleted stayed where it was.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
