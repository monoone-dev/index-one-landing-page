*Originally released on 2026-10-16.*

When the notes you pinned turn out to be empty, Ask Ivy now searches your other notes. Answers made on this Mac keep going when one part of your notes cannot be read in time.

### Ask Ivy

- If every note you pinned is empty, Ivy searches the rest of your notes as a regular question would, with the same locked folders and folder choice. If nothing matches, it says the pinned note is empty instead of saying you have no meeting notes yet.
- If you ask from a shared note that is empty or no longer available to you, Ivy says so instead of answering from your own notes.
- These replies appear in your interface language wherever answers are shown, including reopened threads and quick search.

### Ivy on this Mac

- A question that runs too long on this Mac no longer holds up the next one.
- If one part of your notes cannot be read in time, Ivy skips that part, answers from the rest, and says that some material was not read.
- Voice commands that Ivy cannot classify quickly fall back to the regular command handling.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
