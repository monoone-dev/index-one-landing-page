### Memory and briefs

- Review useful observations, including decisions and commitments, alongside quotes from their sources. Correct or close observations as things change.
- Organize people with aliases and relationships.
- Refresh briefs as source material changes and keep your own notes separate from the generated brief.
- Recover and manage briefs, attachments and corrections more reliably, including when items are moved to or restored from Trash.
- In a web Space, review personal memory and open its source evidence.

Access to memory and its evidence is checked against the current source and privacy settings.
