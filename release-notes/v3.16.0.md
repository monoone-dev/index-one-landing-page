*Originally released on 2026-11-11.*

IndexOne has one new look on the Mac and on the web, records straight into an organization, and can write and translate notes in the language you choose.

### A new look

- Surface is now the only look, on the Mac and on the web. The Studio, Paper and Minimalist looks are gone.
- The Mac window is see-through over the sidebar. Set how much with Window transparency in Settings › Appearance.
- Every Settings page has the same layout: a title, then sections of rows in one box.
- Ask Ivy shows the conversation and its history side by side. Drag the edge of the history to resize it; the width is remembered on this Mac.

### Recording to an organization

- The Record screen has a Saves to row. Choose On this Mac, Personal or a space in one of your organizations before you start.
- A badge says where the transcript and the note are made: on this Mac or on the IndexOne server.
- You can record your voice and your screen for a space that is processed on the server.
- IndexOne shows how long you can record. It warns you before the maximum length, and when your storage is about to fill up.
- The recorder bar shows where the recording is saved, the same badge and the time left.
- If a recording could not be sent, it stays on this Mac. The Library lists these recordings with the reason, and you can send them or keep them here. Most are sent again by themselves when the problem is gone.
- Meetings in an organization show a badge while the server is still making the transcript and the note.
- The first time you record, IndexOne reminds you to tell everyone in the meeting that it is being recorded.

### Organizations

- An organization on the Mac has the same screens as On this Mac: recording, the space tree, meetings and notes, related notes, Ask Ivy history, tasks, trash, search, export, rename and making a note again.
- Organization settings is one screen, the same on the Mac and on the web, with tabs for members, spaces and plugins.
- Owners and admins can rename an organization there.
- AI processing is one setting for the whole organization, for every space including new ones. Owners and admins change it. When it is off, a recording saved there gets no transcript or note from the server.

### Signing in

- Signed out, Settings › Account is the sign-in form itself, not a button that opens a dialog. It stays open until you finish, so the recovery phrase of a new account is not cut off.
- Signed in, your account is one row with Sign out.
- On the web, the sign-in screen says that anyone can create an account with their email address.

### Languages

- The new Languages plugin works on the Mac and on the web, for yourself or for a whole organization.
- Choose the language of your notes. Every note Ivy writes comes out in that language, whatever language the meeting was in.
- Add a second language to get a second copy of each note, and see both with Compare with original.
- Translate any meeting later from the language button in the note. The transcript can be translated too.
- Keep a glossary of names and terms that Ivy keeps as they are or translates your way. An organization can have its own.
- Fixed: the language spoken in a meeting is now passed on correctly on the Mac, note images show in the plugin's views, and the language menu fits its content.

### Plugins

- A plugin's page shows where it is installed, by whom and when, with a link to manage it.
- The banner on a plugin's page fills its full width.

### AI settings on the web

- AI settings on the web show only Ivy. The organization owner can turn on private mode; members see the setting but cannot change it.
- The page shows how many of your Ivy tokens you used this month.
- You can set the language of your notes there.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
