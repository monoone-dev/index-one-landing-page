*Originally released on 2026-10-12.*

Locked folders, notes and meetings now share one Touch ID unlock screen, and relocking hides protected content everywhere it was showing.

### Touch ID protection

- Locked folders, notes and meetings open through the same unlock screen, and a locked folder can be unlocked with Touch ID straight from its empty state.
- Unlock progress and errors stay on the item you are unlocking, and a failed unlock shows a clear message instead of a technical error.
- Relocking a folder also locks everything inside it, even when only part of it was open, and lists, titles and other views that showed its contents are refreshed so nothing protected stays visible.

### Meetings and notes

- A tidier meeting toolbar, transcript header and note header.
- The Ivy menu in a note keeps keyboard focus and starts from the first item each time it opens.

### Look and feel

- Search boxes, filter chips, confirmations, menus and page headers across the app use one refreshed set of controls. A second Escape leaves the logs and transcript search boxes, and inline delete confirmations ignore repeated clicks and stray keys.
- Pending briefs stay open, and clicking a brief's header opens its schedules.
- Analytics hides the meeting count while a loading error is shown, and Retry buttons in briefs and audit can no longer fire twice.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
