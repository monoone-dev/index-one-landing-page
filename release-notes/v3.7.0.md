*Originally released on 2026-10-18.*

Signing in and resetting your password are calmer and clearer. A few wrong codes now mean a short pause instead of a day-long lock, and every error tells you what happened and what you can do next.

### Signing in and resetting your password

- After several wrong codes from your email or your authenticator app, IndexOne asks you to wait a few minutes — at most 15 minutes for email codes and 60 minutes for authenticator codes — instead of locking you out until the next day.
- The pause screen shows when you can try again, and offers a way forward right there: use your recovery phrase if your account has one, or write to support.
- Starting over without a recovery phrase now happens at once, instead of after a seven-day wait.
- Each sign-in, account and reset problem has its own clear message, and your email address is accepted in any letter case.

### Polish

- Account screens in Polish no longer address you in a masculine or feminine form.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
