*Originally released on 2026-11-06.*

Work in your organizations from your Mac: the notes, recordings, tasks and Ivy of your IndexOne account now open in the app.

### Organizations on your Mac

- Sign in to your IndexOne account and pick where you work from the menu at the top of the sidebar: Personal or one of your organizations. Personal keeps "On this Mac" apart from "Account personal", the personal content of your account.
- Each organization shows its spaces, the same ones you see on IndexOne on the web. "All notes" and "Recordings and files" list everything you can open there.
- Use "Refresh access" in the toolbar when someone changes what you can see.

### Manage an organization

- Create an organization or join one by accepting an invitation, from the same menu.
- Owners and admins invite teammates by email, choose their role and the spaces they can open, and change or remove access later.
- Create spaces for the whole organization or restrict a space to the people you invite.

### Notes, recordings and tasks

- Read and edit an organization's notes. Move a note to the trash and restore it. If someone else changed a note while you were editing, IndexOne keeps your draft and lets you pick a version.
- Record a meeting into an organization with your microphone and, if you like, system audio, or import audio and documents. The recording stays encrypted on your Mac until you agree to the upload terms and upload it.
- Play recordings, read their text and download them.
- See tasks by space and status, edit their title, description, due date and linked sources, and move them to the trash or back.
- "Move to organization or Personal" moves a note, recording, file or task to another organization or space, with its transcript, notes and history. If a move is interrupted, IndexOne keeps it on your Mac so you can retry it.

### Ivy for organizations

- "Search and Ask" searches all spaces you can open, or one of them, and lets you ask Ivy about them. Answers show the passages they come from.
- Conversations are saved, so you can come back to them later or delete them.
- Ivy for organizations runs in the cloud and asks for your cloud processing permission first.

### Appearance

- A new look, Surface, is in Settings › Appearance. It turns opaque when "Reduce transparency" is on in macOS.

### IndexOne on the web

- While a recording waits for its transcript, the processing label says why: speech recognition is starting, no computing capacity is free, a usage limit is reached, or the organization's plan doesn't include that step. Your recording and text are kept, and you can keep working.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
