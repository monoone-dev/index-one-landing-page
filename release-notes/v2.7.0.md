*Originally released on 2026-09-07.*

Two things in this release, and they pull in the same direction: making the note a place you can
actually sit and work in. One changes what the app is made of, the other gives a note somewhere to
keep its follow-ups.

## A Theme skin, and a quiet one called Paper

Settings → Appearance gains a **Theme** section above the light/dark control, with two skins:
**Studio** — the frosted-glass look Murmur has always shipped — and **Paper**.

A skin is a second axis, not a third theme. Light/dark decides how bright the app is; the skin
decides what it is made of. Both work with all three of Light, Dark and System.

**Paper** is a document skin, for people who read and write all day. Glass and the aurora are off.
Surfaces are opaque and warm, borders are hairlines instead of shadows, radii are tighter, easing is
flat, and the accent is an ink blue rather than the indigo that reads as another AI product. The
shell stops floating: the sidebar and the note's tool column are glued to the window and separated by
a single hairline, the way Obsidian and Notion do it.

**It reads and writes the note at one size — 16px/1.7.** The document used to render markdown at one
size and edit at another, so the same paragraph broke at different words and the text shifted every
time you toggled between reading and writing. Under Paper those are typographically identical and the
jump is gone. Studio is unchanged, down to the byte — that jump is a shipped default and changing it
for everyone is a separate decision.

Text contrast was measured off the live cascade rather than eyeballed: Paper clears 4.5:1 on all five
text pairs in both themes.

## Reminders live in the note now

The note had a "New reminder" button and nowhere to see what it had created — a follow-up you made
from a note vanished into the global inbox and the note showed no trace of it.

There is now a **reminders drawer**, opened by a glyph beside Edit/Preview. It creates reminders
anchored to the note you are in and lists the ones already attached to it. The suggestions card goes
back to only staging suggestions, and collapses away entirely when it has none instead of leaving an
empty strip behind.

**A completed reminder can be reopened.** The circle only ever went one way, so ticking something off
by mistake finished it for good. It is a two-way toggle now, and reopening restores the reminder to
the Inbox if it is actually overdue — rather than parking it somewhere active-on-paper and invisible.

**The Reminders page and the drawer are the same row.** The page still drew the old card: a bar of
three text buttons under every row, four lines of chrome for one line of content. Both surfaces now
render one component in the compact Apple-Reminders shape — completion circle, text, and icon actions
that surface on hover.

**Locked notes stay locked.** A reminder attached to a note you have not unlocked this session arrives
with nothing to match, and the drawer does not render for a locked note at all.

## Also fixed

- **Renaming a note reaches the sidebar**, not just the tab. The two read from different places, so a
  note you had just named still said "Untitled" in the tree — exactly when you are looking at it.
- **The note tool pane's header controls take a click on their whole surface.** The window-drag strip
  was hit-testing over the top third of "New reminder": perfectly visible, and dead to a click.
- **Closing the reminders composer no longer loses focus entirely.** This one only ever happened in
  the engine Murmur actually ships — so it was a real bug for every user while most tests were green.
- The note's tool pane is now the same kind of object as the sidebar, and picks up its rounding and
  material in Studio, its flatness in Paper.
- The drawers reach the edge of the content column instead of stopping 24px short of it.
