*Originally released on 2026-11-12.*

Bring your own local model: IndexOne can write notes and answers with Ollama, LM Studio, oMLX or another OpenAI-compatible server. Ivy stays the default, and now shows exactly which model it runs.

### Local AI server

- Settings › AI Models has a new Local AI server section. It is collapsed and off until you open it.
- Enter the server's address, then type a model name or choose Load models and pick one. Set the context allowance, the amount of text the model can read at once, and choose Test connection before you save.
- Ollama, LM Studio, oMLX, llama.cpp and other servers with an OpenAI-compatible address work. Ollama gets your context allowance with every request.
- Once saved, Local AI server is one more choice in AI in this workspace. Notes and @brain answers are then written there. Ivy stays the default.
- With a server on this Mac, nothing leaves it.
- A server elsewhere on your network is used only after you confirm that exact address. Names are masked first, and IndexOne contacts only the address you entered.
- A long meeting is condensed part by part to fit the context allowance, instead of being cut off. The room left for the transcript is measured from the real request, including your notes and related notes.
- A note the server stops at its length limit is reported as incomplete and is not saved.
- The smallest context allowance is 16K, so a note request always fits.

### Ivy

- Ivy now shows its model, quantisation and context window, for example "Qwen3 4B Instruct 2507 · Q4_K_M · 32K context".
- Ivy's size no longer counts one model twice. A light model picked for notes is shown with its real size.
- Older notes written with Ollama say "Ollama on this Mac" instead of "Ivy on this Mac".

### Feedback

- Send a bug or an idea from inside IndexOne with Send feedback in the sidebar. You can add a screenshot of the window, images and, if you choose, technical details. Your notes, recordings and transcripts are never attached.
- Settings › Feedback lists what you sent, where it stands and the team's replies.
- A meeting that can't be opened now says so, confirms nothing was changed, and offers Report this.

### Moving to a new Mac

- If your library is encrypted but its key can't be read, usually after moving to a new Mac, IndexOne still refuses to create a new key, so no meeting becomes unreadable.
- The message now explains what to do: on your previous Mac, create a backup in Settings › Backups with Restorable in IndexOne turned on, then restore it on the new Mac from Settings › Imports. Keep your previous Mac's library until you have checked it.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
