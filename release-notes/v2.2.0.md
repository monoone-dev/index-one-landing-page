*Originally released on 2026-09-01.*

## One sidebar instead of a rail and a panel

The separate icon rail and contextual panel are gone. There is now a single sidebar that
holds everything and collapses to a rail with one click — window buttons, Search, Ask and
Settings sit in a band level with the traffic lights, and Capture keeps its own wide button
in the footer.

**Spaces are now Workspaces.** Same thing, clearer name, and the tree gained a permanent
**Shared** section beside it. The main window also opens 30% larger by default.

## Trash

Deleting a recording or a note no longer means it is gone. Items move to **Trash** and stay
recoverable for 30 days, with the Workspaces tree refreshing itself when you restore one.
Nothing in the app promises permanence on a delete that is now reversible.

## Quick note

**Quick note** sits beside Capture in the sidebar footer. When you create a note, IndexOne asks
where it goes — and can create that place for you if it does not exist yet. The Edit/Preview
toggle is now icon-only and remembers the mode you prefer.

## Fixes

- A Space shared with you arrives **with its contents**, and sharing something with yourself
  renders once rather than twice.
- Settings is back beside the sidebar, along with the pieces the shell rewrite had dropped.

## Also

The landing-page tour was re-cut from the real shipping UI: eighteen shots, a locked camera,
and every claim checked against the code that backs it.
