IndexOne now has a public home: downloads, release notes and docs live at [index-one.io](https://index-one.io) and in this repository. **Update checks now look at this repository. If you are on 2.8.0 or earlier, install this version by hand once** — after that, Settings → About → Check for updates finds new versions again.

### Write and edit in one place

- Notes and meeting notes are a live markdown document: click the text to edit, click outside it to see the preview. The separate Edit/Preview switch is gone.
- Checklists (`- [ ]`) render as real checkboxes you can tick; the change is saved back to the note.
- Pasting or dropping an image into a meeting note puts it where your cursor is.
- Meeting notes get the same Ask Ivy button as notes, and its panel lines up with the note header.

### A new shell

- A slim icon rail on the left for Search, Smart organize, Browse, Ask, Settings and your account, and one top bar with your tabs and an always-visible new-note button.
- Drag the sidebar's right edge to make it wider or narrower (double-click to reset).
- Drag folders, projects, notes and recordings anywhere in the sidebar, including to the top level and back to Unfiled.
- Dashboards are now called Projects everywhere.

### Organizing

- **Smart organize** replaces the old automatic filer: pick notes or recordings and a destination, choose a rule (by day or by how they are linked), review every move, then apply.
- Moving a meeting, note or document uses one hierarchy picker with search and breadcrumbs.
- **Shared with you** separates shared folders and Spaces from individual notes, and shows who shared what and what you can do with it.

### Recording

- While you record, a live panel shows what you and the others are saying.
- When you stop, choose to process the recording now or later. Postponed recordings wait in a queue you can retry or clear.

### Look and feel

- Three skins — Minimalist, Studio and Paper — each in light and dark, rebuilt on one consistent design system.
- Polish letters render in the app's own typeface instead of a fallback font.
- Action-item checkboxes in a meeting now tick the task in the note, and long reminder descriptions are shown in full.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
