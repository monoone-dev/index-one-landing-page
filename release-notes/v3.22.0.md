### Plans and billing

- Buy a plan in fewer steps and see a paid plan badge in your account.
- Accept the terms, privacy policy and withdrawal notice when you buy, and withdraw from a Personal subscription within 14 days from billing settings.
- See which plugins are included in paid plans.
- The terms, privacy policy, withdrawal notice and data processing agreement are published on the web.
- Ivy's message box says that answers come from an AI system.

### Usage and recordings

- Track your plan limits in a card at the bottom of the sidebar, shown as three rings.
- See the remaining cloud transcription time in the recording bar.
- Set up automatic joining for calendar meetings and see upcoming meetings in the meeting bot card.

### Updates

- IndexOne now tells you when an update is recommended or required.

### Downloads

IndexOne requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 13.4 or later.
