*Originally released on 2026-11-11.*

A recording saved to Personal or to an organization space is no longer shown as not sent when it did arrive.

### Recording to an organization

- A recording you saved to Personal or to a space in an organization could be shown as not sent, although it had arrived. It is now shown as saved.
- Nothing was lost: the meeting was in its space all along.
- IndexOne now sends a recording only after this Mac has finished processing it.
- Known issue: a copy of such a recording can still be listed on this Mac. The next update removes it.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
