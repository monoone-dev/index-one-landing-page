*Originally released on 2026-10-24.*

Organization sharing is dependable again: updates arrive on their own, shared documents can be edited from your other Macs, and sharing messages say what to do next.

### Organization sharing

- Updates from your organizations arrive automatically when IndexOne starts, after you unlock it, when the connection comes back and when you return to the window. Several organizations take turns, so one busy organization no longer holds up the others.
- A slow download of a large shared file keeps the progress it has made instead of starting over.
- You can edit a document you shared from another Mac signed in to the same account again. Drafts and earlier revisions are kept.
- A saved change is no longer reported as failed when only the refresh after it failed.
- IndexOne tells a remembered account apart from an unlocked sharing session, and explains what you need to do to continue.
- Sharing errors now say whether you lack permission, the document no longer exists, it changed in the meantime or it is too large.

### Notes

- When you delete a note from the ⋯ menu, keyboard focus moves to the Delete confirmation again.

### Security

- Updated components that had published security advisories.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
