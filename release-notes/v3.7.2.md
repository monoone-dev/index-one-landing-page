*Originally released on 2026-10-19.*

Notes for very long meetings now get the time they need, and a meeting whose note failed can try again without transcribing the whole recording again.

### Notes

- When a cloud AI provider writes the note, IndexOne gives it more time for longer meetings, up to 30 minutes for the longest ones.
- If the provider runs out of time, IndexOne says so in plain words.
- A meeting whose transcript is ready but whose note failed shows **Generate note again**. It uses the saved transcript and does not offer to recover the recording again.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
