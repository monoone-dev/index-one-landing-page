*Originally released on 2026-11-11.*

Recordings now live where you saved them, plugins work only in the places you install them, and you choose which workspaces AI apps can read.

### Recordings live where you saved them

- A recording saved to Personal or to a space in an organization appears in that space right away: while it records, while this Mac processes it and while it is being sent. Before, it was listed under On this Mac until it arrived.
- You can open it there before it arrives: the note, the transcript and the audio come from this Mac until the space has it. It is never listed twice.
- Each recording shows one status, such as Sending… or Not sent yet. The reason and Send now appear only when you have to do something, on the meeting and inside it.
- Sending again is done in the space. The Not sent yet section of the Library and the Unsent recordings card on Record are gone.
- The organization menu shows a count next to an organization with recordings that wait for you.
- On this Mac shows only meetings that are not in any organization: the meetings list, tags, search, the sidebar, people, the graph, dashboards and reminders.
- To take a recording out of its organization, choose Move to On this Mac in the meeting's menu. It then stays only on this Mac.
- A recording you can't open in its organization, for example because you signed out, stays under On this Mac with its status, so it never disappears.

### Recording

- The Saves to row on Record is one line: where it saves, the processing badge and the time left.
- Its menu lists every organization, with one row per space and a check on the one you chose. Personal and On this Mac are single rows, and Personal is no longer written twice.
- An organization with no space you can record to is shown greyed out, with the reason and a link to Spaces & access.
- Signed out, the menu explains why only this Mac is offered. When your storage is full it says so.
- If a recording could not be sent, the record controls stay usable.
- A recording with no speech says "Nothing to send — no speech was recorded" and stays on this Mac.

### Plugins

- When you install a plugin, choose where it works: On this Mac, Personal or an organization. It runs only where it is installed, and each place has its own settings.
- The Record screen uses the plugins of the place you record to, and a meeting uses the plugins of the place it lives in.
- When you switch organization, the plugins and settings of the previous one are gone at once.
- Plugins now turn on as soon as you are signed in. Before, they stayed off until sharing was unlocked.

### Organizations and Ask

- The Browse menu shows the same items in every organization. An item an organization does not have yet stays in the menu and says why.
- Notes in an organization list that organization's notes, and a new note is created there.
- The / menu in an organization note no longer lists titles from Personal.
- Ask in an organization says it is still being prepared, with progress, instead of finding nothing while this Mac gets it ready.
- Ask with Ivy on this Mac reads more of your notes and can find something that was only said in a transcript.

### AI apps and privacy

- The local MCP server settings have a switch for each workspace. Organizations are off until you turn them on, and an AI app reads one only when it names it. If an AI app used an organization before, turn that organization on.
- Before IndexOne starts a program for an MCP server, or uses a new path to Claude Code, macOS asks you to confirm.
- Links in notes open in your browser. The IndexOne window stays on IndexOne, and web pages inside it can't use the microphone or camera.
- Tags of meetings in a locked folder stay hidden until you unlock it.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
