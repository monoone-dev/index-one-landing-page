*Originally released on 2026-10-07.*

A sharing-account release: you can now stop a password reset you didn't ask for, change your password in Settings, and reset an account that has no recovery phrase.

### Sharing account

- When a password reset is requested for your account, the app shows a notice with the date it takes effect and a button to cancel it. The notice also appears while sharing is locked; you then cancel with your current password.
- Settings → Account can change your sharing password. Your account key, your shares and your saved recovery phrase stay as they are.
- An account without a recovery phrase can now be reset. Before anything is scheduled, the app explains what the reset removes: it takes effect after 7 days, notes on this Mac stay, shares in either direction can no longer be opened, and the organizations you belong to freeze. You confirm it explicitly.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
