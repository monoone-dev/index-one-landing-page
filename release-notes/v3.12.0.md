*Originally released on 2026-11-07.*

Your organizations now open in the same screens as Personal: notes, meetings, Ivy, tasks and recording look and work the same wherever you are.

### One app for Personal and your organizations

- Notes, meetings, Ivy, tasks and recording in an organization use the same screens as Personal. Organization content comes from your IndexOne account, so it needs an internet connection.
- The menu at the top of the sidebar groups where you work into Personal, Organizations and Private groups.
- The Personal sidebar no longer has a separate Shared section.
- Rename an organization's notes and spaces right in the note editor and from the rename item in the sidebar.

### Recordings in an organization

- A meeting you record in an organization goes to the space you choose, and IndexOne on the web makes its transcript and note. If sending fails, you can try again.
- Delete an organization's meetings.
- Settings › AI models shows where an organization's recordings are processed. IndexOne remembers the choice for each organization. Processing on your Mac is marked "soon".

### Fixes

- The spacing under the sidebar menu is even again.
- An empty note form no longer keeps you from leaving it, and "Discard changes" really leaves.
- Old links to organization pages open the organization's notes.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
