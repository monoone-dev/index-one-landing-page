*Originally released on 2026-10-05.*

A bug-fix release: meeting notes work with Codex again, and the note editor's formatting bar can sit where you prefer.

### Codex

- Meeting notes generated with Codex work again with Codex CLI 0.160. After updating Codex, notes failed to generate.
- When processing a meeting fails, the reason is now written to the app's log, without note titles or vault paths.

### Note editor

- A new Editor section in Settings chooses where the formatting bar sits: in the top bar, as before, or as a floating dock at the bottom of the note.
- Headings are grouped in one Headings menu that now goes down to H6, and the bar adds a new-section template and buttons to increase or decrease the indent.
- Ask Ivy in the formatting bar is an icon at its far right, the same in notes and meeting notes.
- The note title and body no longer show a blue frame while you edit.
- The meeting header no longer shows which AI provider wrote the note.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
