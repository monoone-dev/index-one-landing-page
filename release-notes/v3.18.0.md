*Originally released on 2026-11-13.*

Folders come to organization spaces and to IndexOne on the web, the web shows how much of your monthly recording time and Ivy tokens you have used, plugins get their own screen, and replies to your feedback can now carry formatting and images.

### Folders in organization spaces

- Spaces in an organization can hold folders, and folders inside folders, just like the folders on this Mac. Create, rename, move and delete them from the space's tree.
- On the web you can browse a space by folder and create, rename, move and delete folders there too.
- Create a note straight in a folder, or move a note to another folder in the same space.
- If you can only view a space, you see its folders but can't change them.
- A note you write while offline keeps the folder you chose until it is sent.

### Your monthly usage on the web

- The sidebar shows how much recording time and how many Ivy tokens you have used this month, next to your storage. Hover over a meter to see the details.
- When your recording time for the month is used up, the Record screen says so. The limit renews on the 1st, and audio you already saved is kept.
- If your Ivy usage can't be loaded, you can reload it from the sidebar.

### Plugins

- Plugins have their own screen. Open it with the new Plugins button in the left rail, on the Mac and on the web. Each plugin's own settings stay in Settings.
- Old links to plugins in Settings take you to the new screen.
- If you are signed out, the plugins screen asks you to sign in instead of saying it couldn't reach IndexOne.

### Feedback

- Replies from the team under your reports show their formatting, such as lists, links and emphasis, and any images attached to them.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
