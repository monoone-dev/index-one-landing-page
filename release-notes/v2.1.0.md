*Originally released on 2026-08-29.*

## Share a whole Space or folder

Until now you shared one note at a time. Now you can hand your organisation a
**whole Folder or Space** — its notes, its meeting notes, its sub-folders — with
one permission that covers everything inside it.

- **The folder stays live.** A note you add to a shared folder publishes itself.
  Move one out, or delete it, and it goes away for everyone too.
- **View only or Can edit**, chosen once for the whole container. "Can edit"
  covers the text of the notes inside it; the structure stays yours.
- **Arrange what you receive, privately.** Drag a shared Space into your own and
  it stays where you put it. Nobody else sees your arrangement, and the content
  keeps updating from its owner.
- **Locked folders are refused, not silently skipped.** A sealed sub-folder is
  left behind and named in the preview before anything leaves your Mac.
- Transcripts and audio are never shared — a meeting travels as its note.

Nothing about this reaches the server in the clear: container structure rides
inside the same end-to-end-encrypted envelope as the content.

## Shared Brains moved into the sidebar

The separate Shared Brains tab is gone. A Space someone shares with you now
appears as its own row in **Spaces**, and anything shared without a folder lives
in one **Shared Brains** Space alongside it. Shared rows carry a small marker
naming the organisation and your access.

## Unfiled notes are an inbox again

Notes you never filed were showing up inside a folder-shaped row called "Notes"
where rename, delete and lock were all missing — it looked broken. They now sit
in an **Unfiled notes** inbox next to Unfiled recordings.

## Fixes

- The local MCP server for Claude no longer fails silently when it cannot bind.
- MCP resolves a shared task by the id the app shows you.
- Drag-and-drop reaches the page in the packaged app.
- A shared sibling no longer blocks converting a whole folder.
- Conversion refusals say what to do instead of "try again".
- Item headers expose a copyable ID.
- Filing a recording is one workflow across Spaces, and a sealed recording's move
  stays safe.
