*Originally released on 2026-10-16.*

You can now choose whether IndexOne itself shows up when you record your entire screen.

### Screen recording

- A new Hide IndexOne from recordings switch for entire-screen recording, in the source picker's Entire screen tab and in Settings → Screen Recording. It is on by default, as before.
- With it off, the main IndexOne window is recorded, so a recording of a screen showing only IndexOne is no longer black. The recording bar and other IndexOne overlays are never recorded.
- The switch takes effect at once, also during a recording.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
