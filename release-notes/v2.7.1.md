*Originally released on 2026-09-08.*

A patch release that unwedges organization sync.

## Sync now no longer hangs — and no longer takes the rest of the app with it

Pressing **Sync now** in Settings › Organizations deadlocked the app's organization subsystem. The command took an internal lock and then, still holding it, called a routine that takes the same lock again — so it waited on itself forever. It never returned, and every organization operation afterwards (sharing, joining, unlocking a locked folder) blocked with no error and no timeout. The only way out was to quit and reopen IndexOne.

If you have pressed Sync now and then found sharing quietly stopped working, that was this.

## Sync actually catches up now

A manual sync used to fetch four items and report itself finished. A shared Space therefore arrived a few objects at a time, minutes apart — the Space first, its folders later, the notes later still — while the button said "up to date" at every step.

- **Sync now** keeps fetching until the organization is caught up, and says so honestly when it stops early: "More is still arriving in the background."
- The **background sync** now catches a backlog up in seconds instead of four objects a minute, and goes quiet again once it is done.
- The button shows what it is doing — `Syncing… 24`, then `Syncing folders…` — instead of a spinner you cannot tell apart from a hang.

## Signing in refreshes your organizations

Unlocking sharing with Touch ID restored your session but asked the server nothing, so the sidebar kept whatever it had read before you signed in — often until you restarted the app. Signing in now refreshes your organizations and the sidebar straight away.

## Settings no longer says you are in no organization when it just could not check

If reading your organizations failed, the panel showed "You're not in any organization yet" — to people who belong to several — and switching tabs and back was the only way to retry. A failed read now says so, keeps the organizations it already knew about on screen, and retries underneath.
