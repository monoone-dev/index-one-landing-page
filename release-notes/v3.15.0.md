*Originally released on 2026-11-09.*

IndexOne on the Mac now works with the same organizations as IndexOne on the web, keeps a local copy of every web space, and asks one simple question per space: which AI should work here.

### Organizations

- Organizations on the Mac are the organizations you have on the web. They open after you sign in, without a separate unlock.
- The space switcher is a panel: On this Mac, Personal and your organizations, each with one short line saying where its notes live.
- The settings group for your account and organization is now called Organization, on the Mac and on the web.

### Your shared notes are kept

- The old end-to-end encrypted sharing with people, share links and the old encrypted organizations are retired.
- Everything that was shared with you, and every old encrypted organization on this Mac, moves into On this Mac › Shared: folders, notes, meetings, transcripts, recordings and files. Nothing is deleted by sync.
- IndexOne tells you once where your shared notes went. If some recordings could not be kept, it says how many.
- Settings › Organization › Account lists your old share links under Old shared links, so you can stop any that are still live.

### Web spaces on your Mac

- Personal and every organization you belong to keep an encrypted copy on this Mac. They open without a network connection.
- Edits you make while offline are queued and sent when you are back online. If someone changed the same note meanwhile, both versions are kept: theirs in the note, yours in a conflict copy next to it.
- Recordings you play stay on the Mac. Turn on Keep recordings on this Mac to keep all of them.
- Ask, search and the IndexOne MCP server read these copies too, and every answer says which space it comes from.

### One AI choice per space

- Each space has one AI setting: Ivy on this Mac, Ivy on the web (Personal and organizations) or Claude Code or Codex.
- That one choice is used for transcription, notes, Ask, people and topics, facts, translation and the in-meeting assistant.
- Ollama, the Anthropic API, the AI gateway and local Parakeet transcription are removed. Whisper stays for transcription on the Mac.
- The AI Models page shows only the AI of the current space and the Ivy model download.

### Storage

- Your organization's storage view is now in Settings › Organization › Account.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
