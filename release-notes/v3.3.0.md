*Originally released on 2026-10-10.*

Touch ID now guards your folders, notes, meetings and the whole app, and the editor turns what you paste into Markdown right where you type.

### Touch ID protection

- Folders, projects, workspaces, single notes and meetings can be protected with Touch ID. Their contents are encrypted and hidden until you unlock them, and they lock again on their own after a period of inactivity. A side panel sets it up.
- The whole app can lock itself with Touch ID after idle time or as soon as screen sharing starts. Locking the app also locks every folder you had unlocked, and tab titles of protected items are masked.
- Removing protection asks for a fresh Touch ID, and protected items cannot be shared.

### Editor and notes

- Pasted rich text is converted to Markdown and shown formatted in place, including tables, attachment images and long pastes.
- Tab indents lists and moves between table cells, and stays a normal Tab in plain fields.
- Preview opens straight away, without waiting for the note to save.
- A Lock switch in the note menu keeps a note read-only, with a padlock beside the menu while it is on.

### Obsidian

- Obsidian export has been removed. An existing export vault is moved into IndexOne once, and IndexOne no longer writes to it. Importing from Obsidian stays available in Settings.

### Ask and AI

- Ask keeps the relevant passages and late corrections from large notes and several documents, reads broad selections in stages and tells you when an answer covers only part of the material.
- Citations stay intact inside narrow answer tables.
- Edits and translations that the AI cut off, or that no longer match their source, are rejected, so the original and earlier translations are kept.

### Look and feel

- A refreshed set of controls across the app: charts and stats, the recording indicator, trees, quick search, the onboarding and account steps, meeting tags and every dropdown.
- The recording bar can be dragged by any part and has a close button. Note and meeting tool panels share one side panel, and Settings uses one button style.
- Recording timers and live-transcript times show hours, and the recording chart shows the last 30 days correctly across daylight-saving changes.
- The floating bar and meeting detail show translated processing labels.
- Pressing Enter on "Show password" no longer submits sign-in, and a tag that fails to save is removed again.
- Window buttons line up with the side rail.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
