*Originally released on 2026-10-16.*

IndexOne can now record your screen alongside your voice, and plays the video back in sync with the transcript.

### Screen recording

- Next to Start recording, on the recording bar and from the menu bar, choose voice only or voice + screen.
- A source picker with live thumbnails lists your screens, windows from every desktop and apps, and remembers the last source you used.
- While recording you can hide the screen or switch to another source; the voice keeps recording either way.
- A new Recording tab plays the screen video in sync with the audio, with a Screen lane on the timeline, click-to-seek and a small player you can dock, shrink to a pill or close.
- Save video exports the screen video together with the meeting audio.
- Screen videos stay on your Mac like audio. In locked folders they are encrypted along with the timeline of window titles.
- Screen video counts toward storage, and old videos are pruned before audio.
- Settings → Screen Recording sets the default mode, 720p or 1080p, 15 or 30 fps, shows a size estimate and the permission status. Screen recording needs macOS 14 or later; on older systems the option is hidden.

### Fixes

- The update check opens the right release page, and shows a message when the page cannot be opened.
- The recording bar no longer repeats menu-bar recording events.
- Search shows "no matches" only after the search has answered, and the live transcript says "1 line" instead of "1 lines".
- A Smart Organize preview no longer expires the moment it opens on some dates.
- Ivy's answers carry the Ivy logo.

### Installer

- The DMG opens as a branded installer window: drag IndexOne onto Applications.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
