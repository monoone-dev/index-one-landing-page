*Originally released on 2026-10-30.*

Share recordings and files end-to-end encrypted, and see your screen in meeting notes.

### Sharing recordings and files

- Share a meeting's audio and screen recordings and its transcript with people and organizations. Everything is end-to-end encrypted, and IndexOne shows upload progress for each file.
- Notes can hold any file: images and file links sit inline, with previews for images, PDFs, audio, video and text.
- Shared files stay encrypted on the recipient's Mac and open only in IndexOne. A recipient can save a file to disk only when you allow it.
- Take access back from My shares, for a whole share or a single file. Revoked files disappear for the recipient.
- Your inbox shows a shared note's files before you accept it, and tells files you can save apart from view-only ones.
- In organization documents, members who can edit can add files. Leaving an organization removes the files you added there.

### Screen in meeting notes

- When a meeting has a screen recording, IndexOne picks the moments the screen mattered and adds a few frames from them to the note as images.
- Turn it off with "Screen in notes" in Settings. It is on by default.
- Sending images to a note model now has a new consent text, so IndexOne asks for your consent again.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0, IndexOne offers this update itself.
