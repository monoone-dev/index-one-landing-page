*Originally released on 2026-10-05.*

**IndexOne 3.0 — Murmur is now IndexOne.** This version installs as **IndexOne.app** next to Murmur.app and opens the same data: your notes, recordings, folders, settings and Keychain keys stay where they are. The two apps can't run at the same time, and once IndexOne has started you can delete Murmur.app.

### Moving to the new Ivy engine

- After the update a notice asks for a one-time migration in Settings → About → Ivy engine migration. It copies your keys in the Keychain to the names the new engine uses and updates notes that still use the old format. Your text stays the same.
- A backup can come first, and it is optional: every note, transcript, document and image is saved as .md files in an index-one.backup folder you choose. Locked folders are always left out and stay locked.
- Locked folders stay locked through the migration and still open only with Touch ID. Nothing in your Obsidian vault is moved.
- Settings → Health Check shows the migration next to the database, vault, models, AI provider and other-apps connection checks.

### Sharing

- After the update, sign in to your sharing account with your password once. Touch ID unlocks sharing from then on.
- Links and shared notes from earlier versions keep opening, and what you share now still opens for people on an earlier version.

### Organize

- Smart organize takes an instruction and reorganizes the whole library: it creates, renames and moves folders, Workspaces and Projects, shows every change in a preview, applies only what you accept and undoes it in one click.
- After a reorganization, new notes and recordings follow the same rules: first as suggestions you review, then automatically once you turn it on in Settings → Notes.

### Ask Ivy

- Brain is now Ivy, with its own icon and an Ask Ivy button on notes and meeting notes.
- Ask reads the images in a note: text is recognized on this Mac, and an image is sent to the model only after a separate consent.
- Ask history is no longer wiped when an unrelated note is deleted, when a recording discards its empty note, or after an update.
- Selecting text in a note or a meeting note shows the Ask Ivy button again, and its panel opens beside the selection.

### Recording

- Stopping a recording offers "process later" next to "create the note now", in the app, the bar and the tray. The queue can process everything at once and says why a recording failed.
- Stopping a long recording no longer stalls the app.

### Backups and imports

- Settings → Backups saves everything to an index-one.backup folder at any time, with progress and a list of earlier backups. A restorable backup brings back the same Spaces, Projects, folders, notes and meetings through Settings → Imports.
- Imports also bring in any folder of loose .md files.

### Fixes

- Meeting notes work with Codex again: a signed-in Codex was reported as not recognized.
- When the microphone, screen and system audio, or calendar is not allowed, its Health Check row offers Allow or Open System Settings.
- Notices at the top of the window stack instead of covering each other and the tabs.
- A new IndexOne logo with a dark version, in the app icon, onboarding, sharing and Settings → About.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
