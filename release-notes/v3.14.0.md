*Originally released on 2026-11-08.*

IndexOne now shows how much storage your account uses and helps you free it up.

### Storage

- Settings has a new Storage page. It shows how much of your storage you use, by category and by organization, and lists your files, largest or newest first.
- Select files and delete them, or empty the trash in one step. Deleting files never deletes notes or transcripts.
- Choose how long recording files are kept. After that time they are deleted automatically; transcripts, notes and attachments stay.
- The sidebar shows a small storage meter.
- When you reach 95% of your storage, IndexOne tells you. When your storage is full, new uploads and recordings to your account pause until you free up space.

### Organization storage

- An organization's settings show how much storage it uses and how much of it you uploaded.
- Owners and admins also see who uploaded what, sorted by size or name.

### Deleted files

- When a recording, screen recording, image or attachment is deleted, its note now shows that it was deleted, when and by whom, instead of a broken file.

### Screen recording on the web

- IndexOne on the web can now record your screen together with your voice, from a browser tab or the entire screen, in a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer.
- If the screen can't be saved, for example because your storage is full, your voice keeps recording.
- The Screen recording plugin is listed on the web and on the Mac.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
