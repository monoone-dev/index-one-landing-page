One thing in this release, and it answers a question the Related picker in 2.5.0 raised: linking a
folder tells you where something belongs, but it does not make an answer come from what is inside it.
Now you can ask for that directly.

## Ask a question inside one Space or folder

Pick a Space or folder in the Ask **Sources** picker and the answer is drawn only from what is filed
under it — that folder, its sub-folders, and everything in them.

It narrows the **search** rather than stuffing the folder into the question, which is what makes it
usable on real folders: a Space holding a hundred notes costs the same as one holding five, and
relevance still decides what actually gets used inside it.

**It stays current on its own.** A note you write into that folder tomorrow is in scope the next time
you ask — nothing to re-pick, nothing to refresh. A sub-folder you create is included. A note you move
out drops out. Renaming the folder or moving it elsewhere changes nothing, because the scope follows
the folder itself, not its path.

That is the practical difference from picking individual notes as sources: those are a fixed list, and
the next note you write is invisible to it until you add it by hand.

**Locked content stays locked.** A scope can only ever narrow what you could already see — it is never
a way in. Scoping to a folder you have not unlocked this session returns nothing, not its contents.
And a scoped question stays inside its scope even when nothing matches: it will tell you it found
nothing there rather than quietly answering from the rest of your vault.

You can still pin individual notes alongside a scope; those are always included.
