*Originally released on 2026-09-14.*

The meeting view stops being a pile of panels. Everything that *acts* on a meeting now lives in the title row; everything that is a *view* of it opens as one right-docked drawer.

### The meeting command row

- Every action moved into the title row and became icon-only. No label was deleted — each stays in the DOM as screen-reader text, so the accessible name is unchanged.
- Action items, Live context, Smart reminders and Ask were four independent toggles pretending to be independent. The shell only ever holds one open drawer, so they are now a **segmented switcher** that says so — choosing one retires the other, and "nothing open" stays a real state.
- All fourteen icon controls got a real tooltip, replacing the native `title` that waited a second and a half, drew in the system's style, and was unreachable from the keyboard.

### Sidebar

One row rhythm for the whole column. The destination rows, the workspace and shared trees, and the "View all" continuation were authored in three different places and had drifted to three different heights, pitches and label offsets; they now share one. The unfiled "View all" also got back the nesting step it had lost.

### Notes

The **Properties** card above every note body is gone — the tags row, the typed property widgets and *+ Add property*. Front-matter itself is untouched: a note's `tags:` and properties still parse on load and serialize on save, so nothing is stripped from the `.md` files in your vault.

### Fixed

- **Paper skin: the tab strip was dead to clicks.** Neither the close glyph nor the `+` responded, because the window-drag band overlapped the strip and won every hit test. The strip's controls now rank above it.
