*Originally released on 2026-10-21.*

Automatic filing is more dependable, the cloud permission wording is clearer, and secure connections use updated certificate trust.

### Automatic filing

- New notes are filed consistently: IndexOne keeps your chosen filing mode and AI matching after a new organization plan, and waits for a note to be saved before it files it.
- Editing one note while IndexOne files a group of notes no longer stops the others from being filed. They wait for a fresh answer instead.

### Privacy

- The filing card now explains that withdrawing cloud permission stops new requests, while one already on its way may still finish.

### Security

- Secure connections use updated certificate trust.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
