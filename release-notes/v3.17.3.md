*Originally released on 2026-11-12.*

A redesigned Feedback screen: every report now has a number and a title, and you can follow its status and the team's reply in one place, on the Mac and on the web.

### Feedback

- Settings › Feedback lists your reports next to the one you selected, with All, In progress and Finished filters.
- Every report gets a permanent number, such as `IO-B-41`, that you can copy and quote. After you send a report, IndexOne shows you its number.
- Reports now have a title. Older reports without one show their first sentence instead.
- The status uses words that fit the report: a bug is Fixed, an idea is Shipped, anything else is Done. When a fix ships, the report says which version has it, and on the Mac whether you already have it or need to update.
- The team's reply appears as a message under your report.
- Dates follow the language of the app, with the full date when you hover over them.
- In a narrow window, the list and the report are shown one above the other.

### Downloads

The DMG is signed and notarized by Apple. It runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.4 or later. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt` with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`. If you run 3.8.0 or later, IndexOne offers this update itself.
