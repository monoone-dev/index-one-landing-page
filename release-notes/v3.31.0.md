*Originally released on 2026-11-27.*

### New

- On Mac, open Settings → Imports to import a meeting transcript. Choose a local folder or leave the meeting unfiled. IndexOne opens the meeting and starts writing its note after import.
- In cloud workspaces, choose whether to keep original recording files.

### Downloads

IndexOne requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 13.4 or later.
