*Originally released on 2026-11-27.*

### New

- Keep a recording in the cloud. Use Keep recording to keep the original recording of a meeting under your account's storage settings. If the original has already been deleted, IndexOne tells you so.

### Fixes

- Recordings you already had in the cloud stay available while the new storage settings roll out, and items already in the trash are not removed on a timer by this update.
- The Keep recording action stays visible on narrow screens.

### Downloads

IndexOne requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 13.4 or later.
